# The Art Factory Website

Static website for **The Art Factory**, a Mumbai-based custom nameplate and sign business.

## Product system

### 1. Debossed
- Construction: two-layer acrylic
- Front layer: laser-cut acrylic
- Backing layer: coloured acrylic that provides the letter colour
- LED: not available
- Plate colours: Black, White, Matte Black
- Letter/backing colours: Gold, Silver, Rose Gold
- Shapes: Rectangle, Round, Capsule
- Standard sizes: 8 × 6, 12 × 6, 16 × 8, 24 × 8 inch
- Custom size: Contact Us
- Name/Main Text: required
- House/Flat Number: optional
- Design: four fixed production designs, each with its own border
- Other colours/designs/shapes/logos/custom requirements: Contact Us

### 2. Embossed
- Materials: Acrylic or Glass
- Acrylic: raised acrylic letters, LED available, diamond not available
- Acrylic plate colours: Black, White, Matte Black, Red, Royal Blue, Navy Blue, Forest Green, Wine Red/Burgundy, Grey, Cream/Ivory; premium mirror/metal finishes include Gold Mirror, Silver Mirror, Rose Gold Mirror and Copper Mirror
- Acrylic letter colours: Gold, Silver, Rose Gold, White, Black, Red, Royal Blue, Navy Blue, Forest Green, Wine Red/Burgundy; premium Copper, Champagne Gold, Metallic Blue and Metallic Green
- Glass plate colours: Black Glass, White Glass
- Glass letter types: Frosted or Raised Acrylic
- Glass raised acrylic letters: standard acrylic letter colour palette
- Glass diamond backdrop: None, Gold Diamond, Silver Diamond — only with Frosted letters
- LED: No LED, Warm White LED, Cool White LED, RGB LED on Acrylic and Glass
- Shapes: Rectangle, Round, Capsule
- Standard sizes: 8 × 6, 12 × 6, 16 × 8, 24 × 8 inch
- Custom size: Contact Us
- Name/Main Text: required
- House/Flat Number: optional
- Design: four fixed production designs, each with its own border
- Custom materials/finishes/artwork: Contact Us

### 3. Engraved & Charcoal
- Materials: Clear Cast Acrylic or Charcoal Wood
- Shape is determined by the selected design/border
- Standard sizes: 12 × 6, 16 × 8, 24 × 8 inch
- Custom size: Contact Us
- Name/Main Text: required
- House/Flat Number: optional
- Language: English, Hindi, Marathi
- Fonts: five production-tested options per language/script
- Letter colours: Standard Gold, Silver, Rose Gold, White, Black, Red, Royal Blue, Navy Blue, Forest Green, Wine Red/Burgundy; Premium Copper, Champagne Gold, Metallic Blue, Metallic Green
- LED: No LED, Warm White LED, Cool White LED, RGB LED where the selected construction supports it
- Current original templates:
  - Classic Border
  - Botanical Corner
  - Decorative Panel
  - Luxe LED
- Charcoal Wood: raised acrylic lettering
- Clear Cast Acrylic: decorative engraved border construction
- Custom materials, colours, artwork, logos or unusual requirements: Contact Us

## Global production rule
- **Minimum finished letter height: 1 inch / 25.4 mm**
- Production-facing text should be converted/tested as curves before final manufacturing.
- Customer-facing font choices use simple style names rather than technical font-weight terminology.
- Mounting method is not exposed as a customer-selectable option.
